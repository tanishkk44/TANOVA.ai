About

Why TANOVA.ai exists.

TANOVA.ai is a single desk for watching the markets that move fastest — Bitcoin, gold, and the majors in FX — and for turning that noise into a clear, structured plan before you place a trade.

Instead of jumping between a charting app, a news feed and a spreadsheet of open positions, everything sits in one place: live pricing, a model-generated setup for each symbol, and a running journal of every trade you log.

It's an independent project, built and maintained by one person, and it keeps growing with more markets, sharper setups and a better trading journal over time.
